How this page works

The trained Keras model was converted to TensorFlow.js and is served from this repository. When you press Classify, the image is preprocessed with OpenCV.js using the same operations as the training pipeline — retinal crop, 3×3 median filter, CLAHE on the L channel of LAB (clip 2.0, 8×8 tiles), Ben Graham background subtraction (σ = 10), grey padding to a square and resize to 224×224 — then passed through the network. Inference runs on your own device via WebGL; no image ever leaves your browser.

Held-out test set (n = 519): accuracy 0.7341, quadratic weighted kappa 0.7783, referable-disease ROC AUC 0.958. Model weights are stored in docs/model/.

Decision support only — not a diagnosis. Screening aid trained on APTOS 2019; clinical responsibility remains with a qualified practitioner. Not validated on locally acquired images.
